This is a completely new product for private equity investment teams.

Product purpose

Deal Signal is an AI-powered diligence monitoring application that helps investment teams identify:

material changes in deal evidence
contradictions between sources
emerging diligence risks
unanswered questions
potential impact on the investment thesis
recommended follow-up actions

The product should feel like a serious institutional investment intelligence platform, not a generic AI chatbot.

The core idea is that the system is proactive.

Instead of waiting for a user to ask a question, it monitors incoming diligence evidence and surfaces important signals automatically.
