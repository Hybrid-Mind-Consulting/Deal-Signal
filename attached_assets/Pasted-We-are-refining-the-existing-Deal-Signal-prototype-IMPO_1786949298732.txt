2. SLOW DOWN “SIMULATE NEW EVIDENCE”
--------------------------------------------------

The current Simulate New Evidence interaction completes too quickly.

The purpose of this feature is to demonstrate that Deal Signal is continuously analysing new evidence rather than simply displaying a pre-generated result.

The process must be slow enough for a person watching a live demo to understand each stage.

When the user clicks:

“Simulate New Evidence”

run the following staged sequence.

Total duration should be approximately 8–10 seconds.

Use approximately 1–1.3 seconds between meaningful stages.

The sequence should be:

STEP 1
NEW EVIDENCE DETECTED

July Management Accounts — Aug 2026

Supporting text:
“New financial evidence received”

STEP 2
EXTRACTING KEY DATA

Supporting text:
“Customer A revenue: £12.4m”
“Total Q2 revenue: £40.0m”

STEP 3
CALCULATING CUSTOMER CONCENTRATION

Supporting text:
“£12.4m ÷ £40.0m = 31.0%”

Important:
Make this visibly a deterministic calculation, not an AI-generated number.

STEP 4
CHECKING RELEVANT PRIOR EVIDENCE

Supporting text:
“Searching connected diligence evidence”

STEP 5
PRIOR EVIDENCE IDENTIFIED

Supporting text:
“Management Presentation — May 2026”
“Previous understanding: 18%”

STEP 6
CROSS-SOURCE COMPARISON

Supporting text:
“18% → 31%”
“Variance: +13 percentage points”

STEP 7
MATERIALITY ASSESSMENT

Supporting text:
“Assessing investment relevance and evidence quality”

STEP 8
HIGH-PRIORITY SIGNAL DETECTED

Supporting text:
“Customer concentration has increased materially”
“Commercial · High materiality · 94% confidence”
